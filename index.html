Nine ways to speak your offer into being

The practices from the post you saved, turned towards the one thing you want right now: a job offer.

  1. 01

    Speak in present tense

    Not "I will get a job." Say "I am a working engineer."

  2. 02

    Replace negatives

    Drop "nobody is hiring." Say "The right company is hiring me."

  3. 03

    Thank before you receive

    Every night: "Thank you for my offer letter."

  4. 04

    Use power words

    Hired. Selected. Confident. Prepared. Grateful. Use them in how you talk about yourself.

  5. 05

    Affirm aloud daily

    Your voice makes it real. Say three affirmations out loud each morning.

  6. 06

    Cancel and replace

    Catch yourself saying "I'm stuck"? Say "Cancel that. I am moving forward."

  7. 07

    Speak as if you're heard

    Talk about your future the way you'd want an interviewer to hear it.

  8. 08

    Repeat with emotion

    Picture the offer email arriving. Feel it while you speak.

  9. 09

    Declare your identity

    Not "looking for work." Say "I am a DevOps and data professional."

Affirmations for every part of you

Pick the card that fits your mood today and read it aloud, line by line.

Confidence

  • I believe in myself completely.
  • I speak with power and clarity.
  • I deserve the success I am creating.
  • My experience makes me stand out.

Interviews

  • I enjoy talking about my work.
  • I stay calm when questions get hard.
  • Interviewers like me and trust me.
  • I turn every interview into an offer.

Learning

  • Concepts click for me easily.
  • I remember what I study.
  • I practise until I master it.
  • I am becoming an expert, one hour at a time.

Money and abundance

  • Money flows to me from my work.
  • I am paid well for the value I bring.
  • My bank balance grows every month.
  • I have more than enough.

Resilience

  • Setbacks make me stronger.
  • I rise every time I fall.
  • Rejection is redirection.
  • I never give up on myself.

Gratitude

  • Thank you for my new job.
  • Thank you for my health and energy.
  • Thank you for my family's love.
  • Thank you for this fresh start.

Peace of mind

  • I release worry and breathe slowly.
  • Everything is working out for me.
  • I sleep well and wake up hopeful.
  • I am safe, supported and guided.

My future

  • I see myself joining my new team.
  • I see the offer email in my inbox.
  • I see myself growing year after year.
  • My best days are ahead of me.
