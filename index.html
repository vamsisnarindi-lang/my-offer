Nine ways to speak your offer into being

The practices from the post you saved, turned towards the one thing you want right now: a job offer.

  1. 01

    Speak in present tense

    Not "I will get a job." Say "I am a working engineer."

  2. 02

    Replace negatives

    Drop "nobody is hiring." Say "The right company is hiring me."

  3. 03

    Thank before you receive

    Every night: "Thank you for my offer letter."

  4. 04

    Use power words

    Hired. Selected. Confident. Prepared. Grateful. Use them in how you talk about yourself.

  5. 05

    Affirm aloud daily

    Your voice makes it real. Say three affirmations out loud each morning.

  6. 06

    Cancel and replace

    Catch yourself saying "I'm stuck"? Say "Cancel that. I am moving forward."

  7. 07

    Speak as if you're heard

    Talk about your future the way you'd want an interviewer to hear it.

  8. 08

    Repeat with emotion

    Picture the offer email arriving. Feel it while you speak.

  9. 09

    Declare your identity

    Not "looking for work." Say "I am a DevOps and data professional."
